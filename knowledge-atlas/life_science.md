# 生命科学核心原理 / Core Principles of Life Sciences

## 学科定位 / Scope

生命科学研究生命的结构、功能、遗传、演化、发育以及生物与环境的关系。

Life sciences study the structure, function, heredity, evolution, development of living systems, and their relationships with the environment.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 细胞 | Cell | 生命结构和功能的基本单位；the fundamental structural and functional unit of life. |
| 稳态 | Homeostasis | 生物维持内部环境相对稳定；maintenance of relatively stable internal conditions. |
| 代谢 | Metabolism | 维持生命的全部化学反应；the complete set of chemical reactions sustaining life. |
| 遗传信息 | Genetic Information | 主要编码在 DNA 序列中的可遗传信息；heritable information primarily encoded in DNA sequences. |
| 基因表达 | Gene Expression | 遗传信息转化为功能产物；conversion of genetic information into functional products. |
| 蛋白质 | Protein | 执行催化、结构、运输和信号等功能的大分子；macromolecules performing catalytic, structural, transport, and signaling roles. |
| 细胞信号 | Cell Signaling | 细胞接收、处理并响应信息；cells receive, process, and respond to information. |
| 遗传 | Heredity | 性状和遗传信息在世代间传递；transmission of traits and genetic information across generations. |
| 变异 | Variation | 个体之间可遗传或非遗传的差异；heritable or non-heritable differences among individuals. |
| 自然选择 | Natural Selection | 差异生存繁殖改变群体性状频率；differential survival and reproduction changes trait frequencies. |
| 生态系统 | Ecosystem | 生物群落与非生物环境形成的系统；a system formed by communities and their abiotic environment. |
| 共生 | Symbiosis | 不同物种之间长期密切关系；a close, long-term relationship between species. |

## 生命的统一原理 / Unifying Principles of Life

- 细胞学说 / Cell Theory
- DNA—RNA—蛋白质的信息流 / DNA-RNA-protein information flow
- 能量转换与物质循环 / Energy transformation and matter cycling
- 结构与功能相适应 / Structure relates to function
- 稳态依赖反馈调节 / Homeostasis depends on feedback regulation
- 演化解释生命的统一性与多样性 / Evolution explains unity and diversity
- 生物系统具有多层次涌现性质 / Biological systems exhibit multilevel emergence

- 对照实验 / Controlled Experiment
- 测序 / Sequencing
- 基因编辑 / Genome Editing
- 培养与模型生物 / Culture and Model Organisms
- 系统发育分析 / Phylogenetic Analysis

## 关键对照 / Key Comparisons

- 基因型与表型 / Genotype vs. Phenotype
- 有丝分裂与减数分裂 / Mitosis vs. Meiosis
- 原核与真核 / Prokaryote vs. Eukaryote
- 个体发育与物种演化 / Development vs. Evolution
- 相关与因果 / Correlation vs. Causation

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

普通生物学 → 细胞与生物化学 → 遗传与分子生物学 → 生理与发育 → 演化与生态 → 专业方向。

General biology → cell biology and biochemistry → genetics and molecular biology → physiology and development → evolution and ecology → specialization.
