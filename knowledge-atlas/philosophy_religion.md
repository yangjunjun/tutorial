# 哲学与宗教学核心原理 / Core Principles of Philosophy and Religious Studies

## 学科定位 / Scope

哲学以论证研究知识、存在、价值、心灵和推理；宗教学以历史、比较和社会科学方法研究宗教现象。

Philosophy uses argument to study knowledge, existence, value, mind, and reasoning. Religious studies examines religion through historical, comparative, and social-scientific methods.

## 知识地图 / Knowledge Map

形而上学 / Metaphysics、认识论 / Epistemology、逻辑学 / Logic、伦理学 / Ethics、政治哲学 / Political Philosophy、心灵哲学 / Philosophy of Mind、科学哲学 / Philosophy of Science、美学 / Aesthetics、宗教史 / History of Religions、比较宗教学 / Comparative Religion.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|

| 健全性 | Soundness | 有效且前提实际为真的论证；a valid argument with true premises. |

## 研究原则 / Research Principles

- 区分前提、推理和结论 / Distinguish premises, inference, and conclusion.
- 善意重构最强版本观点 / Apply the principle of charity.
- 区分描述性事实与规范性判断 / Separate descriptive facts from normative judgments.
- 宗教学研究不等同于信仰认同或反对 / Religious studies is not the same as endorsing or rejecting belief.
- 概念清晰先于争论 / Conceptual clarity precedes productive disagreement.

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

逻辑与哲学导论 → 认识论、伦理学、形而上学 → 哲学史与世界宗教 → 专题文本和独立论证。

Logic and introduction → epistemology, ethics, and metaphysics → history of philosophy and world religions → specialized texts and independent argument.
