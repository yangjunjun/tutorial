# 经济学核心原理与概念 / Core Principles and Concepts of Economics

## 文档定位 / Purpose

经济学（Economics）研究个人、企业、政府和社会如何在资源稀缺的条件下进行选择，以及这些选择如何通过市场、制度和政策相互影响。

Economics studies how individuals, firms, governments, and societies make choices under scarcity, and how those choices interact through markets, institutions, and public policy.

## 1. 经济学基础 / Foundations of Economics

### 1.1 稀缺性 / Scarcity

稀缺性是指可用资源不足以满足所有欲望，因此选择不可避免。

Scarcity means that available resources are insufficient to satisfy every possible want, making choice unavoidable.

### 1.2 选择 / Choice

经济选择是在多个可行方案之间分配有限资源。任何选择通常都意味着放弃其他可能。

Economic choice allocates limited resources among feasible alternatives. Choosing one option generally means giving up others.

### 1.3 机会成本 / Opportunity Cost

机会成本是为了获得某项选择而放弃的最佳替代方案的价值。

Opportunity cost is the value of the best alternative forgone when a choice is made.

### 1.4 权衡 / Trade-Off

权衡表示改善一个目标可能需要牺牲另一个目标，例如效率与公平、当前消费与未来消费。

A trade-off means improving one objective may require sacrificing another, such as efficiency versus equity or present versus future consumption.

### 1.5 边际分析 / Marginal Analysis

边际分析比较多做一个单位所带来的额外收益与额外成本。

Marginal analysis compares the additional benefit and additional cost of one more unit of an activity.

基本决策规则 / Basic decision rule:

```text
若边际收益 > 边际成本，则增加活动
If Marginal Benefit > Marginal Cost, increase the activity.
```

### 1.6 激励 / Incentive

激励是改变行为收益或成本的因素。价格、税收、罚款、补贴、声誉和制度规则都可能构成激励。

An incentive is a factor that changes the benefits or costs of behavior. Prices, taxes, fines, subsidies, reputation, and institutional rules can all create incentives.

### 1.7 理性选择 / Rational Choice

传统模型通常假设决策者在约束条件下选择最符合自身目标的方案。理性不等于自私，也不要求掌握全部信息。

Traditional models often assume decision-makers choose the option that best serves their objectives under constraints. Rationality does not necessarily mean selfishness or perfect information.

### 1.8 经济人 / Homo Economicus

经济人是一个简化模型：主体拥有相对稳定的偏好，并在约束下进行一致选择。

Homo economicus is a simplifying model in which agents have relatively stable preferences and make consistent choices under constraints.

### 1.9 资源配置 / Resource Allocation

资源配置研究土地、劳动、资本、技术和时间如何在不同用途之间分配。

Resource allocation studies how land, labor, capital, technology, and time are distributed among competing uses.

### 1.10 生产可能性边界 / Production Possibility Frontier, PPF

生产可能性边界表示在既定资源和技术下，一个经济体能够生产的两种产品的最大组合。

A Production Possibility Frontier shows the maximum combinations of two goods an economy can produce with given resources and technology.

它可以说明 / It illustrates:

- 稀缺性 / Scarcity
- 机会成本 / Opportunity Cost
- 生产效率 / Productive Efficiency
- 经济增长 / Economic Growth

### 1.11 实证经济学与规范经济学 / Positive and Normative Economics

- 实证经济学研究“是什么”以及变量之间的因果或统计关系。
- 规范经济学讨论“应该是什么”，包含价值判断。

Positive economics studies what is and examines causal or statistical relationships. Normative economics discusses what ought to be and involves value judgments.

### 1.12 微观与宏观经济学 / Microeconomics and Macroeconomics

- 微观经济学研究个人、家庭、企业和具体市场。
- 宏观经济学研究总产出、就业、通胀、增长和整体政策。

Microeconomics studies individuals, households, firms, and particular markets. Macroeconomics studies aggregate output, employment, inflation, growth, and economy-wide policy.

## 2. 经济学研究方法 / Economic Methodology

### 2.1 模型 / Model

经济模型有意简化现实，以突出关键变量、机制和可检验关系。

An economic model deliberately simplifies reality to highlight key variables, mechanisms, and testable relationships.

### 2.2 假设 / Assumption

假设限定模型适用范围。假设是否有用，取决于它能否帮助解释和预测目标现象，而不只是是否完全符合现实。

Assumptions define a model's scope. Their usefulness depends on whether they help explain and predict the target phenomenon, not merely on whether they are perfectly realistic.

### 2.3 其他条件不变 / Ceteris Paribus

“其他条件不变”表示分析某个变量变化时，暂时保持其他相关因素不变。

Ceteris paribus means holding other relevant factors constant while examining the effect of one variable.

### 2.4 均衡 / Equilibrium

均衡是各参与者在给定条件下都没有单方面改变行为动机的状态。

An equilibrium is a state in which no participant has an incentive to change behavior unilaterally under the given conditions.

### 2.5 静态、比较静态与动态分析 / Static, Comparative-Static, and Dynamic Analysis

- 静态分析研究某一时点的均衡。
- 比较静态分析比较外部条件变化前后的均衡。
- 动态分析研究变量随时间调整的路径。

Static analysis studies equilibrium at a point in time. Comparative statics compares equilibria before and after an external change. Dynamic analysis studies adjustment paths over time.

### 2.6 内生变量与外生变量 / Endogenous and Exogenous Variables

- 内生变量由模型内部机制决定。
- 外生变量由模型外部给定。

Endogenous variables are determined within a model. Exogenous variables are taken as given from outside the model.

### 2.7 相关与因果 / Correlation and Causation

相关表示变量共同变化；因果表示一个变量的变化会导致另一个变量变化。相关本身不能证明因果。

Correlation means variables move together. Causation means a change in one variable produces a change in another. Correlation alone does not establish causation.

### 2.8 反事实 / Counterfactual

反事实是“如果没有某项政策、事件或处理，结果会怎样”的假想状态，是因果推断的核心。

A counterfactual is the hypothetical outcome that would have occurred without a policy, event, or treatment. It is central to causal inference.

### 2.9 局部均衡与一般均衡 / Partial and General Equilibrium

- 局部均衡聚焦单个市场，并暂时忽略其他市场反馈。
- 一般均衡同时分析多个市场和主体之间的相互影响。

Partial equilibrium focuses on one market while holding cross-market feedback aside. General equilibrium studies interactions among many markets and agents simultaneously.

## 3. 供给、需求与市场均衡 / Supply, Demand, and Market Equilibrium

### 3.1 需求 / Demand

需求描述消费者在不同价格下愿意且能够购买的数量。

Demand describes the quantities consumers are willing and able to purchase at different prices.

### 3.2 需求定律 / Law of Demand

在其他条件不变时，商品价格上升通常导致需求量下降，价格下降通常导致需求量上升。

Other things equal, a higher price generally reduces quantity demanded, while a lower price generally increases it.

### 3.3 需求量变化与需求变化 / Change in Quantity Demanded vs. Change in Demand

- 商品自身价格变化引起沿需求曲线移动。
- 收入、偏好、预期或相关商品价格变化会使整条需求曲线移动。

A change in the good's own price causes movement along the demand curve. Changes in income, preferences, expectations, or related-good prices shift the entire curve.

### 3.4 供给 / Supply

供给描述生产者在不同价格下愿意且能够出售的数量。

Supply describes the quantities producers are willing and able to sell at different prices.

### 3.5 供给定律 / Law of Supply

在其他条件不变时，价格上升通常增加供给量，因为额外生产变得更有吸引力。

Other things equal, a higher price generally increases quantity supplied because additional production becomes more attractive.

### 3.6 市场均衡 / Market Equilibrium

市场均衡发生在供给量等于需求量时，对应均衡价格和均衡数量。

Market equilibrium occurs where quantity supplied equals quantity demanded, determining an equilibrium price and quantity.

### 3.7 短缺与过剩 / Shortage and Surplus

- 价格低于均衡水平时可能出现短缺。
- 价格高于均衡水平时可能出现过剩。

A price below equilibrium may create a shortage. A price above equilibrium may create a surplus.

### 3.8 替代品与互补品 / Substitutes and Complements

- 替代品可以相互替换，一种商品涨价可能提高另一种商品需求。
- 互补品通常共同消费，一种商品涨价可能降低另一种商品需求。

Substitutes can replace one another, so a price increase for one may raise demand for the other. Complements are consumed together, so a price increase for one may reduce demand for the other.

### 3.9 正常品与低档品 / Normal and Inferior Goods

- 收入增加时需求上升的是正常品。
- 收入增加时需求下降的是低档品。

Demand for a normal good rises with income. Demand for an inferior good falls with income.

### 3.10 价格弹性 / Price Elasticity

价格弹性衡量数量对价格变化的敏感程度。

Price elasticity measures how responsive quantity is to a change in price.

```text
需求价格弹性 = 需求量变动百分比 ÷ 价格变动百分比
Price Elasticity of Demand =
Percentage Change in Quantity Demanded ÷ Percentage Change in Price
```

### 3.11 收入弹性与交叉价格弹性 / Income and Cross-Price Elasticity

- 收入弹性衡量需求对收入变化的反应。
- 交叉价格弹性衡量一种商品需求对另一种商品价格变化的反应。

Income elasticity measures demand responsiveness to income. Cross-price elasticity measures demand responsiveness for one good to the price of another.

### 3.12 价格上限与价格下限 / Price Ceiling and Price Floor

- 有约束力的价格上限低于均衡价，可能造成短缺。
- 有约束力的价格下限高于均衡价，可能造成过剩。

A binding price ceiling lies below equilibrium and may create a shortage. A binding price floor lies above equilibrium and may create a surplus.

## 4. 消费者理论 / Consumer Theory

### 4.1 偏好 / Preferences

偏好描述消费者如何对不同消费组合进行排序。

Preferences describe how a consumer ranks alternative consumption bundles.

### 4.2 效用 / Utility

效用是表示消费者偏好满足程度的模型工具，不一定是可直接测量的幸福值。

Utility is a modeling device representing preference satisfaction; it need not be a directly measurable quantity of happiness.

### 4.3 总效用与边际效用 / Total and Marginal Utility

- 总效用是消费一定数量获得的总体满足。
- 边际效用是多消费一个单位带来的额外效用。

Total utility is overall satisfaction from consumption. Marginal utility is the additional utility from consuming one more unit.

### 4.4 边际效用递减 / Diminishing Marginal Utility

在其他条件不变时，连续消费同一种商品通常会使额外一单位带来的效用逐渐下降。

Other things equal, consuming successive units of the same good generally reduces the additional utility from each extra unit.

### 4.5 预算约束 / Budget Constraint

预算约束表示消费者在收入和价格既定时能够购买的消费组合。

A budget constraint represents the consumption bundles affordable at given income and prices.

### 4.6 无差异曲线 / Indifference Curve

无差异曲线连接给消费者相同效用水平的消费组合。

An indifference curve connects consumption bundles that provide the same utility level.

### 4.7 边际替代率 / Marginal Rate of Substitution, MRS

边际替代率表示消费者愿意放弃多少一种商品来换取额外一单位另一种商品，同时保持效用不变。

The Marginal Rate of Substitution is the amount of one good a consumer is willing to give up for one more unit of another while holding utility constant.

### 4.8 消费者最优 / Consumer Optimum

消费者最优是在预算约束下使效用最大化的可行消费组合。

The consumer optimum is the affordable bundle that maximizes utility under the budget constraint.

### 4.9 收入效应与替代效应 / Income and Substitution Effects

- 替代效应来自相对价格变化。
- 收入效应来自购买力变化。

The substitution effect arises from a change in relative prices. The income effect arises from a change in purchasing power.

### 4.10 显示性偏好 / Revealed Preference

显示性偏好通过消费者实际选择推断其偏好，而不是依赖主观陈述。

Revealed preference infers preferences from observed choices rather than subjective statements.

## 5. 生产者理论 / Producer Theory

### 5.1 生产函数 / Production Function

生产函数描述投入要素与最大可实现产出之间的技术关系。

A production function describes the technological relationship between inputs and the maximum attainable output.

### 5.2 生产要素 / Factors of Production

传统分类包括：

Traditional categories include:

- 土地 / Land
- 劳动 / Labor
- 资本 / Capital
- 企业家才能 / Entrepreneurship
- 技术与知识 / Technology and Knowledge

### 5.3 短期与长期 / Short Run and Long Run

- 短期至少有一种投入固定。
- 长期所有投入都可以调整。

In the short run, at least one input is fixed. In the long run, all inputs can be adjusted.

### 5.4 边际产量 / Marginal Product

边际产量是增加一单位投入带来的额外产出。

Marginal product is the additional output produced by one more unit of an input.

### 5.5 边际报酬递减 / Diminishing Marginal Returns

在其他投入固定时，持续增加某一可变投入，其边际产量最终可能下降。

When other inputs are fixed, continually increasing one variable input may eventually reduce its marginal product.

### 5.6 固定成本与可变成本 / Fixed and Variable Costs

- 固定成本不随当前产量变化。
- 可变成本随产量变化。

Fixed costs do not vary with current output. Variable costs change with output.

### 5.7 平均成本与边际成本 / Average and Marginal Cost

- 平均成本是总成本除以产量。
- 边际成本是多生产一个单位带来的额外成本。

Average cost is total cost divided by output. Marginal cost is the additional cost of producing one more unit.

### 5.8 规模经济与规模不经济 / Economies and Diseconomies of Scale

- 规模经济表示长期平均成本随产量扩大而下降。
- 规模不经济表示长期平均成本随产量扩大而上升。

Economies of scale occur when long-run average cost falls as output expands. Diseconomies of scale occur when it rises.

### 5.9 显性成本与隐性成本 / Explicit and Implicit Costs

- 显性成本涉及直接货币支付。
- 隐性成本是自有资源的机会成本。

Explicit costs involve direct monetary payments. Implicit costs are the opportunity costs of owner-supplied resources.

### 5.10 会计利润与经济利润 / Accounting and Economic Profit

- 会计利润等于收入减显性成本。
- 经济利润还要减去隐性成本。

Accounting profit equals revenue minus explicit costs. Economic profit also subtracts implicit costs.

### 5.11 利润最大化 / Profit Maximization

在标准模型中，企业选择使总收入减总成本最大的产量；内部解通常满足边际收益等于边际成本。

In the standard model, a firm chooses output to maximize total revenue minus total cost. An interior optimum typically satisfies marginal revenue equal to marginal cost.

## 6. 市场结构 / Market Structures

### 6.1 完全竞争 / Perfect Competition

完全竞争市场有大量买卖双方、同质产品、自由进入退出和充分信息，单个企业是价格接受者。

A perfectly competitive market has many buyers and sellers, homogeneous products, free entry and exit, and sufficient information. Each firm is a price taker.

### 6.2 垄断 / Monopoly

垄断市场只有一个主要卖方，并存在显著进入壁垒。垄断者面对市场需求曲线，通常拥有定价能力。

A monopoly has one dominant seller and significant barriers to entry. The monopolist faces the market demand curve and generally has pricing power.

### 6.3 垄断竞争 / Monopolistic Competition

垄断竞争具有许多企业和差异化产品，企业拥有有限定价能力，但长期仍面临进入竞争。

Monopolistic competition has many firms selling differentiated products. Firms possess limited pricing power but face entry in the long run.

### 6.4 寡头 / Oligopoly

寡头市场由少数大型企业主导，每家企业的决策都会显著影响其他企业。

An oligopoly is dominated by a small number of large firms whose decisions significantly affect one another.

### 6.5 进入壁垒 / Barriers to Entry

进入壁垒阻止或提高新企业进入市场的成本。

Barriers to entry prevent or raise the cost of new firms entering a market.

来源 / Sources:

- 规模经济 / Economies of Scale
- 法律许可与专利 / Licenses and Patents
- 网络效应 / Network Effects
- 关键资源控制 / Control of Essential Resources
- 品牌和转换成本 / Brand and Switching Costs

### 6.6 市场势力 / Market Power

市场势力是企业将价格维持在边际成本以上的能力。

Market power is the ability of a firm to sustain price above marginal cost.

### 6.7 价格歧视 / Price Discrimination

价格歧视是在成本差异不能完全解释的情况下，对不同消费者或购买单位收取不同价格。

Price discrimination means charging different prices to consumers or units when cost differences do not fully explain the variation.

### 6.8 自然垄断 / Natural Monopoly

当一个企业服务整个市场的成本低于多个企业分别服务时，可能形成自然垄断。

A natural monopoly may arise when one firm can serve the entire market at lower cost than multiple competing firms.

### 6.9 反垄断政策 / Antitrust Policy

反垄断政策旨在维护竞争，限制反竞争协议、滥用市场支配地位和损害竞争的并购。

Antitrust policy seeks to preserve competition by restricting anticompetitive agreements, abuse of dominance, and mergers that substantially harm competition.

## 7. 福利经济学与市场失灵 / Welfare Economics and Market Failure

### 7.1 消费者剩余 / Consumer Surplus

消费者剩余是消费者愿付价格与实际支付价格之间的差额。

Consumer surplus is the difference between what consumers are willing to pay and what they actually pay.

### 7.2 生产者剩余 / Producer Surplus

生产者剩余是生产者实际获得价格与其最低愿意接受价格之间的差额。

Producer surplus is the difference between the price producers receive and the minimum price they would accept.

### 7.3 总剩余 / Total Surplus

总剩余通常定义为消费者剩余与生产者剩余之和，用于衡量交易创造的总收益。

Total surplus is commonly defined as consumer surplus plus producer surplus and measures the total gains from trade.

### 7.4 帕累托效率 / Pareto Efficiency

如果无法在不使任何人变差的情况下使至少一个人变得更好，则该配置是帕累托有效的。

An allocation is Pareto efficient if no one can be made better off without making someone else worse off.

帕累托效率不等于公平。

Pareto efficiency does not imply fairness.

### 7.5 无谓损失 / Deadweight Loss

无谓损失是税收、市场势力、外部性或其他扭曲导致未能实现的互利交易价值。

Deadweight loss is the value of mutually beneficial trades not realized because of taxes, market power, externalities, or other distortions.

### 7.6 市场失灵 / Market Failure

市场失灵是市场机制无法有效配置资源的情况。

Market failure occurs when market mechanisms do not allocate resources efficiently.

主要来源 / Main sources:

- 外部性 / Externalities
- 公共品 / Public Goods
- 市场势力 / Market Power
- 信息不对称 / Asymmetric Information
- 不完全市场 / Incomplete Markets

### 7.7 外部性 / Externality

外部性是某个行为对未参与交易的第三方产生未被价格充分反映的成本或收益。

An externality is a cost or benefit imposed on a third party that is not fully reflected in market prices.

- 负外部性 / Negative Externality：如污染。
- 正外部性 / Positive Externality：如知识传播。

### 7.8 科斯定理 / Coase Theorem

在产权明确、交易成本很低等条件下，相关方可能通过协商内部化外部性，但最终分配仍受初始产权影响。

With well-defined property rights and sufficiently low transaction costs, parties may bargain to internalize externalities, although distribution still depends on initial rights.

### 7.9 公共品 / Public Good

公共品通常具有：

Public goods are typically:

- 非竞争性 / Non-Rivalry
- 非排他性 / Non-Excludability

### 7.10 公地悲剧 / Tragedy of the Commons

当资源具有竞争性但难以排除使用者时，个体可能过度使用共享资源。

When a resource is rivalrous but users are difficult to exclude, individuals may overuse the shared resource.

### 7.11 搭便车问题 / Free-Rider Problem

如果无法排除未付费者享受公共品，个体可能缺乏主动付费的激励。

When non-payers cannot be excluded from a public good, individuals may lack incentives to contribute voluntarily.

---

## 8. 博弈论与信息经济学 / Game Theory and Information Economics

### 8.1 博弈论 / Game Theory

博弈论研究多个决策者的结果如何取决于彼此策略。

Game theory studies situations in which each decision-maker's outcome depends on the strategies chosen by others.

### 8.2 参与者、策略与收益 / Players, Strategies, and Payoffs

- 参与者是做出决策的主体。
- 策略是可选择的行动方案。
- 收益表示不同策略组合产生的结果价值。

Players are decision-makers, strategies are available plans of action, and payoffs represent the value of outcomes generated by strategy combinations.

### 8.3 占优策略 / Dominant Strategy

无论其他参与者如何选择，始终优于其他策略的策略称为占优策略。

A dominant strategy performs at least as well as every alternative regardless of what other players do.

### 8.4 纳什均衡 / Nash Equilibrium

纳什均衡中，每个参与者的策略都是对其他参与者策略的最佳回应，任何人都没有单方面偏离的激励。

In a Nash equilibrium, each player's strategy is a best response to the others, so no player has an incentive to deviate unilaterally.

### 8.5 囚徒困境 / Prisoner's Dilemma

囚徒困境说明个体理性选择可能产生对所有参与者都较差的集体结果。

The prisoner's dilemma shows how individually rational choices can produce a collectively inferior outcome.

### 8.6 重复博弈 / Repeated Game

当参与者反复互动时，声誉、惩罚和未来收益可能支持一次性博弈中难以维持的合作。

When players interact repeatedly, reputation, punishment, and future payoffs may sustain cooperation that would be difficult in a one-shot game.

### 8.7 信息不对称 / Asymmetric Information

信息不对称表示交易一方掌握的信息多于另一方。

Asymmetric information occurs when one party to a transaction has more information than another.

### 8.8 逆向选择 / Adverse Selection

逆向选择发生在交易前，隐藏信息导致高风险或低质量类型更容易进入市场。

Adverse selection occurs before a transaction when hidden information causes higher-risk or lower-quality types to be disproportionately represented.

### 8.9 道德风险 / Moral Hazard

道德风险发生在交易后，因行为难以观察或责任部分转移，主体可能承担更多风险。

Moral hazard occurs after a transaction when hidden actions or shifted responsibility encourage greater risk-taking.

### 8.10 信号与筛选 / Signaling and Screening

- 信号由信息较多的一方主动发送。
- 筛选由信息较少的一方设计机制来区分类型。

Signaling is initiated by the better-informed party. Screening is designed by the less-informed party to distinguish types.

### 8.11 委托—代理问题 / Principal-Agent Problem

当代理人的目标和信息与委托人不完全一致时，代理人可能不会完全按照委托人利益行动。

A principal-agent problem arises when an agent's goals and information differ from the principal's, causing imperfect alignment.

### 8.12 机制设计 / Mechanism Design

机制设计反向研究应如何制定规则，使自利参与者的行为实现期望结果。

Mechanism design works backward from desired outcomes to rules that induce appropriate behavior from self-interested participants.

## 9. 国民收入核算 / National Income Accounting

### 9.1 国内生产总值 / Gross Domestic Product, GDP

GDP 是一定时期内一个经济体境内生产的最终商品和服务的市场价值。

GDP is the market value of final goods and services produced within an economy during a given period.

支出法 / Expenditure approach:

```text
Y = C + I + G + NX

C = 消费 Consumption
I = 投资 Investment
G = 政府购买 Government Purchases
NX = 净出口 Net Exports
```

### 9.2 最终品与中间品 / Final and Intermediate Goods

GDP 只计入最终品价值，或通过增加值避免重复计算中间品。

GDP counts final goods, or equivalently value added, to avoid double-counting intermediate goods.

### 9.3 名义 GDP 与实际 GDP / Nominal and Real GDP

- 名义 GDP 使用当期价格。
- 实际 GDP 使用基期或链式价格，以分离产量变化与价格变化。

Nominal GDP uses current prices. Real GDP uses base-period or chain-weighted prices to separate output changes from price changes.

### 9.4 GDP 平减指数 / GDP Deflator

GDP 平减指数衡量国内生产的最终商品和服务总体价格水平。

The GDP deflator measures the overall price level of domestically produced final goods and services.

### 9.5 人均 GDP / GDP per Capita

人均 GDP 是 GDP 除以人口，常作为平均经济产出或物质生活水平的粗略指标。

GDP per capita is GDP divided by population and is often used as a rough indicator of average output or material living standards.

### 9.6 GDP 的局限 / Limitations of GDP

GDP 不直接衡量：

GDP does not directly measure:

- 收入分配 / Income Distribution
- 家庭无偿劳动 / Unpaid Household Work
- 闲暇 / Leisure
- 环境损失 / Environmental Damage
- 主观幸福 / Subjective Well-Being
- 产品质量的全部变化 / Every Change in Product Quality

### 9.7 国民收入恒等式 / National Income Identity

在简化封闭经济中，储蓄与投资在核算意义上相等。

In a simplified closed economy, saving equals investment as an accounting identity.

---

## 10. 就业、失业与通货膨胀 / Employment, Unemployment, and Inflation

### 10.1 劳动力 / Labor Force

劳动力通常包括就业者和正在积极寻找工作的失业者。

The labor force generally consists of employed people and unemployed people actively seeking work.

### 10.2 失业率 / Unemployment Rate

```text
失业率 = 失业人数 ÷ 劳动力人数
Unemployment Rate = Unemployed Persons ÷ Labor Force
```

### 10.3 劳动参与率 / Labor Force Participation Rate

劳动参与率衡量劳动年龄人口中属于劳动力的比例。

The labor force participation rate measures the share of the working-age population in the labor force.

### 10.4 失业类型 / Types of Unemployment

- 摩擦性失业 / Frictional Unemployment：求职匹配需要时间。
- 结构性失业 / Structural Unemployment：技能或地区与岗位不匹配。
- 周期性失业 / Cyclical Unemployment：总需求下降造成。
- 季节性失业 / Seasonal Unemployment：随季节规律变化。

### 10.5 自然失业率 / Natural Rate of Unemployment

自然失业率是经济处于正常长期状态时，由摩擦性和结构性因素形成的失业率。

The natural rate of unemployment is the long-run unemployment rate generated by frictional and structural factors under normal conditions.

### 10.6 通货膨胀 / Inflation

通货膨胀是总体价格水平持续上升，而不是某一种商品单独涨价。

Inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level, not merely a rise in the price of one good.

### 10.7 消费者价格指数 / Consumer Price Index, CPI

CPI 衡量代表性消费篮子的价格变化。

The Consumer Price Index measures changes in the price of a representative basket of consumer goods and services.

### 10.8 通货紧缩与反通胀 / Deflation and Disinflation

- 通货紧缩是总体价格水平下降。
- 反通胀是通胀率下降，但价格水平仍可能上升。

Deflation is a fall in the general price level. Disinflation is a decline in the inflation rate while the price level may still be rising.

### 10.9 预期通胀 / Expected Inflation

预期通胀会影响工资谈判、合同、利率和价格制定。

Expected inflation influences wage bargaining, contracts, interest rates, and price-setting.

### 10.10 菲利普斯曲线 / Phillips Curve

短期菲利普斯曲线描述通胀与失业之间可能存在的关系，但这种关系会受到预期和供给冲击影响，并非固定不变。

The short-run Phillips curve describes a possible relationship between inflation and unemployment, but it shifts with expectations and supply shocks and is not a fixed trade-off.

---

## 11. 总需求、总供给与经济周期 / Aggregate Demand, Aggregate Supply, and Business Cycles

### 11.1 总需求 / Aggregate Demand, AD

总需求是在不同总体价格水平下，家庭、企业、政府和国外部门计划购买的国内总产出。

Aggregate demand is the total domestic output households, firms, government, and foreign buyers plan to purchase at different price levels.

### 11.2 短期总供给 / Short-Run Aggregate Supply, SRAS

短期总供给描述在部分工资和价格调整缓慢时，企业愿意生产的总产出。

Short-run aggregate supply describes total output firms are willing to produce when some wages and prices adjust slowly.

### 11.3 长期总供给 / Long-Run Aggregate Supply, LRAS

长期总供给主要由劳动、资本、技术和制度等生产能力决定。

Long-run aggregate supply is primarily determined by productive capacity, including labor, capital, technology, and institutions.

### 11.4 潜在产出 / Potential Output

潜在产出是资源正常利用、通胀相对稳定时可持续生产的产出水平。

Potential output is the sustainable level of production when resources are normally utilized and inflation is relatively stable.

### 11.5 产出缺口 / Output Gap

产出缺口是实际产出与潜在产出之间的差异。

The output gap is the difference between actual output and potential output.

### 11.6 经济周期 / Business Cycle

经济周期是经济活动围绕长期趋势发生的扩张和收缩。

The business cycle consists of expansions and contractions in economic activity around its long-run trend.

### 11.7 需求冲击与供给冲击 / Demand and Supply Shocks

- 需求冲击改变计划总支出。
- 供给冲击改变生产成本或生产能力。

Demand shocks change planned aggregate spending. Supply shocks change production costs or productive capacity.

### 11.8 乘数效应 / Multiplier Effect

初始支出变化可能通过收入和再支出过程，引起更大的总需求变化；实际大小取决于储蓄、税收、进口和政策反应。

An initial spending change may generate a larger aggregate-demand change through income and respending. Its size depends on saving, taxes, imports, and policy responses.

### 11.9 自动稳定器 / Automatic Stabilizer

自动稳定器无需新的政策决定，就会随经济状况自动改变支出或税收，例如累进税制和失业保障。

Automatic stabilizers change taxes or spending with economic conditions without new policy decisions, such as progressive taxation and unemployment benefits.

---

## 12. 货币、银行与金融体系 / Money, Banking, and the Financial System

### 12.1 货币 / Money

货币通常具有三项功能：

Money commonly performs three functions:

- 交换媒介 / Medium of Exchange
- 计价单位 / Unit of Account
- 价值储藏 / Store of Value

### 12.2 货币供给 / Money Supply

货币供给是经济中可用于支付和储值的货币性资产总量，不同统计口径包含的资产范围不同。

The money supply is the stock of monetary assets available for payment and value storage. Different measures include different asset categories.

### 12.3 商业银行 / Commercial Bank

商业银行吸收存款、提供支付服务、发放贷款并进行期限和风险转换。

Commercial banks take deposits, provide payment services, issue loans, and transform maturities and risks.

### 12.4 银行资产负债表 / Bank Balance Sheet

- 资产包括贷款、证券和准备金。
- 负债包括存款和借款。
- 资本是资产减负债后的净值。

Assets include loans, securities, and reserves. Liabilities include deposits and borrowing. Capital is the residual value of assets minus liabilities.

### 12.5 信用创造 / Credit Creation

银行发放贷款会同时创造相应存款，但过程受到资本、流动性、风险、监管、贷款需求和货币政策约束。

When banks extend loans, they generally create corresponding deposits, subject to capital, liquidity, risk, regulation, credit demand, and monetary-policy constraints.

### 12.6 中央银行 / Central Bank

中央银行通常负责货币政策、基础货币、支付体系稳定，并在特定制度下承担最后贷款人等职责。

A central bank generally conducts monetary policy, manages the monetary base, supports payment-system stability, and may act as lender of last resort.

### 12.7 利率 / Interest Rate

利率是借用资金的价格，也反映时间价值、通胀预期、信用风险和流动性等因素。

An interest rate is the price of borrowing funds and reflects time value, expected inflation, credit risk, liquidity, and other factors.

### 12.8 名义利率与实际利率 / Nominal and Real Interest Rates

近似关系 / Approximate Fisher relation:

```text
实际利率 ≈ 名义利率 - 预期通胀率
Real Interest Rate ≈ Nominal Interest Rate - Expected Inflation
```

### 12.9 金融中介 / Financial Intermediation

金融中介连接储蓄者和资金需求者，降低信息、匹配、交易和风险管理成本。

Financial intermediaries connect savers and borrowers and reduce information, matching, transaction, and risk-management costs.

### 12.10 系统性风险 / Systemic Risk

系统性风险是单个机构、市场或基础设施的问题通过关联和反馈扩散，威胁整个金融体系的风险。

Systemic risk is the danger that problems in one institution, market, or infrastructure spread through linkages and feedback to threaten the broader financial system.

## 13. 财政政策与货币政策 / Fiscal and Monetary Policy

### 13.1 财政政策 / Fiscal Policy

财政政策通过政府支出、税收和转移支付影响总需求、资源配置和收入分配。

Fiscal policy uses government spending, taxation, and transfers to influence aggregate demand, resource allocation, and income distribution.

### 13.2 扩张性与紧缩性财政政策 / Expansionary and Contractionary Fiscal Policy

- 扩张性财政政策通常增加支出或减少税收。
- 紧缩性财政政策通常减少支出或增加税收。

Expansionary fiscal policy generally raises spending or cuts taxes. Contractionary fiscal policy generally reduces spending or raises taxes.

### 13.3 政府预算赤字与债务 / Government Budget Deficit and Debt

- 赤字是某一时期政府支出超过收入的流量。
- 债务是过去借款累积形成的存量。

A deficit is a flow in which government spending exceeds revenue during a period. Debt is the accumulated stock of past borrowing.

### 13.4 挤出效应 / Crowding-Out Effect

政府借款可能通过提高利率、占用资源或影响预期，减少部分私人投资；程度取决于经济状态和货币环境。

Government borrowing may reduce some private investment through interest rates, resource use, or expectations. The magnitude depends on economic conditions and the monetary environment.

### 13.5 货币政策 / Monetary Policy

货币政策通过政策利率、资产负债表和沟通等工具影响金融条件、总需求和通胀。

Monetary policy uses policy rates, the central-bank balance sheet, communication, and related tools to influence financial conditions, aggregate demand, and inflation.

### 13.6 政策利率 / Policy Rate

政策利率是中央银行用来引导短期市场利率和整体金融条件的重要工具。

The policy rate is a key central-bank instrument for guiding short-term market rates and broader financial conditions.

### 13.7 公开市场操作 / Open-Market Operations

中央银行买卖金融资产，以管理准备金和短期利率。

Open-market operations are central-bank purchases and sales of financial assets to manage reserves and short-term interest rates.

### 13.8 量化宽松 / Quantitative Easing, QE

当短期政策利率受到下限约束时，中央银行可能购买较长期资产，以影响长期利率、资产价格和金融条件。

When short-term policy rates are constrained by a lower bound, a central bank may purchase longer-term assets to influence long-term yields, asset prices, and financial conditions.

### 13.9 货币政策传导 / Monetary-Policy Transmission

政策通过利率、信贷、汇率、资产价格和预期等渠道影响消费、投资、就业和价格。

Policy affects consumption, investment, employment, and prices through interest-rate, credit, exchange-rate, asset-price, and expectations channels.

### 13.10 政策时滞 / Policy Lag

政策存在识别、决策、实施和产生效果的时滞，因此效果可能在经济环境变化后才出现。

Policy faces recognition, decision, implementation, and impact lags, so its effects may appear after economic conditions have changed.

### 13.11 规则与相机抉择 / Rules and Discretion

- 规则强调预先承诺和可预测性。
- 相机抉择允许政策根据实时情况灵活调整。

Rules emphasize commitment and predictability. Discretion allows policy to adapt flexibly to current conditions.

## 14. 经济增长与发展 / Economic Growth and Development

### 14.1 经济增长 / Economic Growth

经济增长通常指实际总产出或人均实际产出长期增加。

Economic growth generally means a sustained increase in real output or real output per person.

### 14.2 劳动生产率 / Labor Productivity

劳动生产率是单位劳动投入产生的产出。

Labor productivity is output produced per unit of labor input.

### 14.3 资本积累 / Capital Accumulation

资本积累通过投资增加机器、设备、基础设施和其他生产性资产。

Capital accumulation increases productive assets such as machinery, equipment, and infrastructure through investment.

### 14.4 人力资本 / Human Capital

人力资本是教育、技能、健康和经验形成的生产能力。

Human capital is productive capacity embodied in education, skills, health, and experience.

### 14.5 技术进步 / Technological Progress

技术进步使相同投入可以产生更多或更高质量的产出，是长期增长的重要来源。

Technological progress enables the same inputs to produce more or better output and is a major source of long-run growth.

### 14.6 全要素生产率 / Total Factor Productivity, TFP

全要素生产率衡量不能简单由资本和劳动数量解释的产出效率。

Total Factor Productivity measures output efficiency not directly explained by quantities of capital and labor.

### 14.7 索洛增长模型 / Solow Growth Model

索洛模型强调资本积累、人口增长和外生技术进步对长期产出路径的影响，并展示资本边际报酬递减。

The Solow model emphasizes capital accumulation, population growth, and exogenous technological progress, while illustrating diminishing returns to capital.

### 14.8 内生增长理论 / Endogenous Growth Theory

内生增长理论把创新、知识、人力资本和研发激励纳入模型内部解释。

Endogenous growth theory explains innovation, knowledge, human capital, and research incentives within the model.

### 14.9 经济发展 / Economic Development

经济发展不仅包括收入增长，还包括健康、教育、制度、机会、能力和生活质量改善。

Economic development includes not only income growth but also improvements in health, education, institutions, opportunities, capabilities, and quality of life.

### 14.10 贫困陷阱 / Poverty Trap

贫困陷阱是低收入通过营养、教育、融资、基础设施或制度等机制自我强化，使个体或地区难以脱离贫困。

A poverty trap is a self-reinforcing mechanism in which low income perpetuates itself through nutrition, education, finance, infrastructure, institutions, or related channels.

### 14.11 制度与发展 / Institutions and Development

产权、法治、治理能力、政治约束和社会信任会影响投资、创新、交易和长期发展。

Property rights, rule of law, state capacity, political constraints, and social trust affect investment, innovation, exchange, and long-run development.

---

## 15. 国际贸易与国际金融 / International Trade and Finance

### 15.1 绝对优势 / Absolute Advantage

如果一个生产者使用更少资源生产某种产品，就在该产品上具有绝对优势。

A producer has an absolute advantage in a good if it can produce it using fewer resources.

### 15.2 比较优势 / Comparative Advantage

如果生产某种产品的机会成本更低，就在该产品上具有比较优势。比较优势是贸易互利的核心原理。

A producer has a comparative advantage in a good if its opportunity cost of production is lower. Comparative advantage is the central principle behind gains from trade.

### 15.3 专业化 / Specialization

依据比较优势进行专业化，可以扩大总产出和潜在消费集合，但收益分配并不必然均等。

Specialization according to comparative advantage can expand total output and possible consumption, although gains need not be distributed equally.

### 15.4 贸易条件 / Terms of Trade

贸易条件是出口价格相对于进口价格的比率，影响一个经济体用出口换取进口的能力。

The terms of trade are the ratio of export prices to import prices and affect how many imports an economy can obtain for its exports.

### 15.5 关税与配额 / Tariff and Quota

- 关税是对进口征税。
- 配额直接限制进口数量。

A tariff is a tax on imports. A quota directly limits import quantity.

### 15.6 贸易保护 / Trade Protection

贸易保护可能支持特定行业或政策目标，但也可能提高消费者价格、造成无谓损失并引发报复。

Trade protection may support selected industries or policy goals, but can also raise consumer prices, create deadweight loss, and invite retaliation.

### 15.7 国际收支 / Balance of Payments

国际收支记录一个经济体与世界其他地区之间的经济交易。

The balance of payments records economic transactions between an economy and the rest of the world.

主要账户 / Main accounts:

- 经常账户 / Current Account
- 资本与金融账户 / Capital and Financial Account

### 15.8 汇率 / Exchange Rate

汇率是一种货币用另一种货币表示的价格。

An exchange rate is the price of one currency in terms of another.

### 15.9 名义汇率与实际汇率 / Nominal and Real Exchange Rates

- 名义汇率比较货币价格。
- 实际汇率根据两国价格水平调整，反映商品相对价格。

The nominal exchange rate compares currency prices. The real exchange rate adjusts for price levels and reflects relative goods prices.

### 15.10 固定汇率与浮动汇率 / Fixed and Floating Exchange Rates

- 固定汇率由政策维持在目标水平或区间。
- 浮动汇率主要由外汇市场供求决定。

A fixed exchange rate is maintained near a target or band by policy. A floating exchange rate is mainly determined by foreign-exchange supply and demand.

### 15.11 购买力平价 / Purchasing Power Parity, PPP

购买力平价认为长期汇率与不同经济体的相对价格水平存在联系，但运输成本、非贸易品和市场摩擦会造成偏离。

Purchasing Power Parity links long-run exchange rates to relative price levels, though transport costs, non-traded goods, and market frictions cause deviations.

### 15.12 不可能三角 / Impossible Trinity

一个经济体无法同时完全实现固定汇率、资本自由流动和独立货币政策，最多选择其中两项。

An economy cannot simultaneously maintain a fixed exchange rate, free capital mobility, and independent monetary policy; at most two can be fully achieved.

## 16. 劳动经济学 / Labor Economics

### 16.1 劳动供给与劳动需求 / Labor Supply and Demand

劳动供给来自个人对工作、收入和闲暇的选择；劳动需求来自企业对劳动边际产出价值的判断。

Labor supply arises from individuals' choices among work, income, and leisure. Labor demand reflects firms' valuation of labor's marginal product.

### 16.2 工资 / Wage

工资是劳动服务的价格，受生产率、技能、制度、议价能力、市场结构和宏观环境影响。

A wage is the price of labor services and is influenced by productivity, skills, institutions, bargaining power, market structure, and macroeconomic conditions.

### 16.3 劳动边际收益产品 / Marginal Revenue Product of Labor

劳动边际收益产品是增加一单位劳动带来的额外收入。

The Marginal Revenue Product of Labor is the additional revenue generated by one more unit of labor.

### 16.4 人力资本投资 / Human-Capital Investment

教育、培训、经验和健康投入可能提高未来生产率和收入，但也有时间、费用和不确定性成本。

Investment in education, training, experience, and health may raise future productivity and earnings but entails time, monetary, and uncertainty costs.

### 16.5 补偿性工资差异 / Compensating Wage Differential

较危险、辛苦或不便利的工作可能需要更高工资，以补偿不利的非工资特征。

Jobs that are riskier, harder, or less convenient may require higher wages to compensate for undesirable non-wage characteristics.

### 16.6 搜寻与匹配 / Search and Matching

劳动力市场匹配需要时间和信息，求职者与岗位的异质性会造成摩擦性失业和职位空缺并存。

Labor-market matching takes time and information. Worker-job heterogeneity can produce simultaneous unemployment and vacancies.

### 16.7 歧视 / Discrimination

劳动市场歧视可能来自偏好、错误统计推断、制度障碍或市场结构，并造成工资和就业机会差异。

Labor-market discrimination may arise from preferences, erroneous statistical inference, institutional barriers, or market structure and can create wage and employment disparities.

### 16.8 工会与集体谈判 / Trade Unions and Collective Bargaining

工会通过集体谈判影响工资、工作条件和劳动规则，其效果取决于制度与市场环境。

Trade unions influence wages, working conditions, and labor rules through collective bargaining, with effects depending on institutions and market conditions.

## 17. 公共经济学 / Public Economics

### 17.1 政府的经济职能 / Economic Roles of Government

常见职能包括：

Common roles include:

- 提供公共品 / Providing Public Goods
- 纠正外部性 / Correcting Externalities
- 维护竞争 / Preserving Competition
- 社会保险 / Social Insurance
- 收入再分配 / Income Redistribution
- 宏观稳定 / Macroeconomic Stabilization

### 17.2 税收 / Taxation

税收为公共支出筹资，同时会改变行为激励和资源配置。

Taxes finance public expenditure while also changing behavioral incentives and resource allocation.

### 17.3 比例税、累进税与累退税 / Proportional, Progressive, and Regressive Taxes

- 比例税的平均税率相对不变。
- 累进税的平均税率随税基增加而上升。
- 累退税对低收入者形成更高的相对负担。

A proportional tax has a roughly constant average rate. A progressive tax has a rising average rate. A regressive tax imposes a larger relative burden on lower-income groups.

### 17.4 法定税负与经济税负 / Statutory and Economic Tax Incidence

法律规定谁缴税，不等于最终谁承担税负。经济税负取决于供需弹性和市场调整。

The party legally required to remit a tax is not necessarily the party that bears its economic burden. Incidence depends on elasticities and market adjustment.

### 17.5 税收效率与公平 / Tax Efficiency and Equity

税制设计通常需要在筹资能力、行为扭曲、行政成本、横向公平和纵向公平之间权衡。

Tax design balances revenue capacity, behavioral distortions, administrative cost, horizontal equity, and vertical equity.

### 17.6 社会保险 / Social Insurance

社会保险通过广泛分担风险，应对失业、疾病、残疾、养老等个人难以完全自行保险的风险。

Social insurance pools risks broadly to address unemployment, illness, disability, retirement, and other risks that individuals may not fully insure privately.

### 17.7 成本收益分析 / Cost-Benefit Analysis

成本收益分析尝试以一致尺度比较政策或项目的社会成本和社会收益。

Cost-benefit analysis compares the social costs and benefits of a policy or project using a consistent metric.

### 17.8 公共选择 / Public Choice

公共选择理论使用经济学方法分析选民、政治家、官僚和利益集团的行为。

Public choice applies economic reasoning to voters, politicians, bureaucrats, and interest groups.

### 17.9 寻租 / Rent Seeking

寻租是通过影响规则或权力分配获取收益，而不是创造新的社会价值。

Rent seeking obtains benefits by influencing rules or power allocation rather than creating new social value.

## 18. 金融经济学 / Financial Economics

### 18.1 货币时间价值 / Time Value of Money

由于投资机会、通胀和风险，今天的一单位货币通常比未来同额货币更有价值。

Because of investment opportunities, inflation, and risk, one unit of money today is generally worth more than the same nominal amount in the future.

### 18.2 现值与终值 / Present and Future Value

```text
终值 Future Value: FV = PV(1 + r)^n
现值 Present Value: PV = FV / (1 + r)^n
```

### 18.3 风险与收益 / Risk and Return

投资者通常要求更高预期收益来承担更高的不可分散风险，但更高风险不保证实际获得更高收益。

Investors generally require higher expected returns for bearing greater non-diversifiable risk, but greater risk does not guarantee a higher realized return.

### 18.4 风险厌恶 / Risk Aversion

风险厌恶者在期望收益相同时更偏好不确定性较低的方案。

A risk-averse person prefers the less uncertain option when expected returns are equal.

### 18.5 分散化 / Diversification

把资金配置到相关性不完全一致的资产，可以降低特定资产风险。

Allocating funds across imperfectly correlated assets can reduce asset-specific risk.

### 18.6 系统性风险与非系统性风险 / Systematic and Idiosyncratic Risk

- 系统性风险影响整个市场，难以通过普通分散化消除。
- 非系统性风险与特定企业或资产有关，可以通过分散化降低。

Systematic risk affects the broad market and cannot be eliminated through ordinary diversification. Idiosyncratic risk is asset-specific and can be reduced through diversification.

### 18.7 有效市场假说 / Efficient Market Hypothesis, EMH

有效市场假说认为资产价格会较快反映可获得信息。不同形式对“哪些信息已被反映”有不同定义。

The Efficient Market Hypothesis states that asset prices incorporate available information relatively quickly. Its forms differ in what information is assumed to be reflected.

### 18.8 资产定价 / Asset Pricing

资产定价研究未来现金流、时间、风险和投资者偏好如何决定资产当前价值。

Asset pricing studies how future cash flows, time, risk, and investor preferences determine present asset values.

### 18.9 债券 / Bond

债券是发行方向持有人承诺未来支付利息和本金的债务工具。

A bond is a debt instrument under which the issuer promises future interest and principal payments.

### 18.10 股票 / Equity

股票代表对企业剩余收益和资产的所有权索取权。

Equity represents an ownership claim on a firm's residual earnings and assets.

### 18.11 衍生品 / Derivative

衍生品价值取决于标的资产、利率、指数或事件，可用于对冲、投机和风险转移。

A derivative derives its value from an underlying asset, rate, index, or event and can be used for hedging, speculation, and risk transfer.

## 19. 行为经济学 / Behavioral Economics

### 19.1 有限理性 / Bounded Rationality

人受到信息、时间和认知能力限制，通常使用简化规则而非完整优化。

People face limits in information, time, and cognitive capacity and often use simplified rules rather than full optimization.

### 19.2 启发式 / Heuristic

启发式是快速决策的经验规则，能够节省认知成本，但可能产生系统性偏差。

A heuristic is a mental shortcut that saves cognitive effort but may generate systematic bias.

### 19.3 损失厌恶 / Loss Aversion

损失厌恶表示同等规模的损失通常比收益带来更强烈的心理影响。

Loss aversion means losses often have a stronger psychological impact than gains of equal size.

### 19.4 前景理论 / Prospect Theory

前景理论描述人们相对于参照点评价收益和损失，并对概率进行非线性加权。

Prospect theory describes how people evaluate gains and losses relative to a reference point and weight probabilities nonlinearly.

### 19.5 锚定效应 / Anchoring

决策可能过度受到最初接触的数字或信息影响。

Decisions may be excessively influenced by an initially encountered number or piece of information.

### 19.6 可得性偏差 / Availability Bias

人们可能根据案例是否容易回忆来判断事件概率，而不是依据完整统计信息。

People may judge probabilities by how easily examples come to mind rather than by complete statistical evidence.

### 19.7 确认偏差 / Confirmation Bias

人们倾向寻找和重视支持既有观点的信息，并忽略相反证据。

People tend to seek and favor information that supports existing beliefs while discounting contrary evidence.

### 19.8 现时偏好 / Present Bias

现时偏好表示人们对即时收益给予不成比例的高权重，可能导致拖延或储蓄不足。

Present bias means people place disproportionate weight on immediate rewards, potentially causing procrastination or insufficient saving.

### 19.9 默认效应 / Default Effect

人们常倾向维持预设选项，因此默认规则会显著影响选择。

People often remain with a preselected option, so defaults can substantially influence decisions.

### 19.10 助推 / Nudge

助推通过改变选择架构影响行为，但不禁止选项或大幅改变经济激励。

A nudge changes choice architecture to influence behavior without banning options or substantially changing economic incentives.

## 20. 制度经济学与产权 / Institutional Economics and Property Rights

### 20.1 制度 / Institution

制度是塑造互动的正式规则、非正式规范及其执行机制。

Institutions are formal rules, informal norms, and enforcement mechanisms that structure interactions.

### 20.2 产权 / Property Rights

产权规定谁可以使用、收益、转让或排除他人使用某项资源。

Property rights define who may use, benefit from, transfer, or exclude others from a resource.

### 20.3 交易成本 / Transaction Cost

交易成本包括搜寻信息、谈判、签约、监督、执行和解决争议的成本。

Transaction costs include the costs of search, negotiation, contracting, monitoring, enforcement, and dispute resolution.

### 20.4 企业边界 / Boundaries of the Firm

企业会比较内部组织与市场交易的成本，从而决定哪些活动自己完成、哪些外包。

Firms compare the costs of internal organization with market exchange when deciding what to perform internally and what to outsource.

### 20.5 契约 / Contract

契约规定参与方的权利、义务和风险分配。现实契约通常是不完全的，无法预先覆盖所有状态。

A contract specifies rights, obligations, and risk allocation. Real-world contracts are usually incomplete and cannot anticipate every possible state.

### 20.6 路径依赖 / Path Dependence

路径依赖表示历史选择会通过学习、协调、转换成本和既得利益影响未来可行方案。

Path dependence means historical choices shape future options through learning, coordination, switching costs, and vested interests.

### 20.7 网络效应 / Network Effect

当产品对一个用户的价值随其他用户数量增加而提高时，存在网络效应。

A network effect exists when a product's value to one user rises as the number of other users increases.

## 21. 环境与资源经济学 / Environmental and Resource Economics

### 21.1 环境外部性 / Environmental Externality

污染、碳排放和生态破坏可能把成本施加给未参与相关交易的人。

Pollution, carbon emissions, and ecological damage may impose costs on people not involved in the underlying transaction.

### 21.2 庇古税 / Pigouvian Tax

庇古税试图按边际外部成本对产生负外部性的活动征税，使私人决策更接近社会成本。

A Pigouvian tax aims to charge an activity according to its marginal external cost, aligning private decisions more closely with social costs.

### 21.3 排放交易 / Emissions Trading

排放交易设定总排放上限，并允许排放许可交易，以市场方式寻找较低成本的减排组合。

Emissions trading sets an overall emissions cap and allows permit trading to discover a lower-cost allocation of reductions.

### 21.4 可再生资源与不可再生资源 / Renewable and Nonrenewable Resources

- 可再生资源可以在适当管理下恢复。
- 不可再生资源的存量在经济时间尺度内有限。

Renewable resources can replenish under appropriate management. Nonrenewable resources have finite stocks over economically relevant time horizons.

### 21.5 贴现率 / Discount Rate

贴现率决定未来成本和收益折算为当前价值时的权重，对长期环境政策评估影响很大。

The discount rate determines the present weight assigned to future costs and benefits and strongly affects long-horizon environmental evaluation.

### 21.6 可持续发展 / Sustainable Development

可持续发展强调在满足当代需求的同时，维护后代满足其需求的能力。

Sustainable development seeks to meet present needs while preserving future generations' ability to meet their own.

## 22. 计量经济学 / Econometrics

### 22.1 计量经济学 / Econometrics

计量经济学结合经济理论、数学和统计方法，使用数据估计关系、检验假设和评估政策。

Econometrics combines economic theory, mathematics, and statistics to estimate relationships, test hypotheses, and evaluate policy using data.

### 22.2 总体与样本 / Population and Sample

- 总体是研究对象的完整集合。
- 样本是从总体中观察到的子集。

A population is the full set of units of interest. A sample is the observed subset drawn from that population.

### 22.3 参数与估计量 / Parameter and Estimator

- 参数是总体中未知的真实量。
- 估计量是用样本数据推断参数的规则。

A parameter is an unknown population quantity. An estimator is a rule for inferring it from sample data.

### 22.4 回归分析 / Regression Analysis

回归分析描述因变量与一个或多个解释变量之间的条件关系。

Regression analysis describes the conditional relationship between a dependent variable and one or more explanatory variables.

### 22.5 普通最小二乘法 / Ordinary Least Squares, OLS

OLS 选择使残差平方和最小的回归系数。

OLS chooses regression coefficients that minimize the sum of squared residuals.

### 22.6 误差项 / Error Term

误差项包含模型未显式解释的因素、测量误差和随机扰动。

The error term captures omitted influences, measurement error, and random disturbances not explicitly represented by the model.

### 22.7 统计显著性 / Statistical Significance

统计显著性衡量观察结果在特定零假设下是否较难由抽样波动解释，但不等于经济重要性。

Statistical significance measures whether an observed result is difficult to explain by sampling variation under a null hypothesis. It does not imply economic importance.

### 22.8 置信区间 / Confidence Interval

置信区间给出一种按重复抽样覆盖目标参数的区间估计方法。

A confidence interval is an interval-estimation procedure designed to cover the target parameter at a stated rate under repeated sampling.

### 22.9 内生性 / Endogeneity

当解释变量与误差项相关时会产生内生性，使普通回归难以解释为因果关系。

Endogeneity occurs when an explanatory variable is correlated with the error term, undermining a causal interpretation of ordinary regression.

来源 / Sources:

- 遗漏变量 / Omitted Variables
- 反向因果 / Reverse Causality
- 测量误差 / Measurement Error
- 同时性 / Simultaneity

### 22.10 随机对照试验 / Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT

随机分配处理使处理组和对照组在平均意义上可比，有助于识别因果效应。

Random assignment makes treatment and control groups comparable on average, helping identify causal effects.

### 22.11 工具变量 / Instrumental Variables, IV

有效工具变量影响处理变量，但除通过该处理外不直接影响结果，并且与相关未观察因素独立。

A valid instrument affects the treatment, has no direct effect on the outcome except through that treatment, and is independent of relevant unobserved factors.

### 22.12 双重差分 / Difference-in-Differences, DiD

双重差分比较处理组和对照组在政策前后的结果变化差异，关键依赖平行趋势等假设。

Difference-in-Differences compares before-after changes in a treatment group with those in a control group, relying critically on assumptions such as parallel trends.

### 22.13 回归不连续 / Regression Discontinuity Design, RDD

当处理由阈值规则决定时，RDD 比较阈值附近个体，以识别局部因果效应。

When treatment is assigned by a cutoff rule, RDD compares units near the threshold to identify a local causal effect.

### 22.14 面板数据 / Panel Data

面板数据在多个时期重复观察相同个体、企业、地区或国家。

Panel data repeatedly observes the same individuals, firms, regions, or countries over multiple periods.

### 22.15 时间序列 / Time Series

时间序列按时间顺序观察同一变量，常涉及趋势、季节性、自相关和非平稳性。

A time series observes a variable over time and commonly involves trend, seasonality, autocorrelation, and nonstationarity.

### 22.16 预测与因果推断 / Prediction and Causal Inference

- 预测关注对未知结果的准确估计。
- 因果推断关注干预会使结果发生怎样的变化。

Prediction focuses on accurate estimates of unknown outcomes. Causal inference focuses on how intervention changes outcomes.

## 23. 不平等、贫困与社会福利 / Inequality, Poverty, and Social Welfare

### 23.1 收入与财富 / Income and Wealth

- 收入是一定时期内获得的资源流量。
- 财富是某一时点资产减负债的存量。

Income is a flow of resources received over a period. Wealth is a stock of assets minus liabilities at a point in time.

### 23.2 收入分配 / Income Distribution

收入分配研究收入在个人、家庭、群体和生产要素之间如何分布。

Income distribution studies how income is distributed among individuals, households, groups, and factors of production.

### 23.3 洛伦兹曲线 / Lorenz Curve

洛伦兹曲线表示累计人口比例与其获得的累计收入或财富比例之间的关系。

The Lorenz curve relates cumulative population shares to cumulative income or wealth shares.

### 23.4 基尼系数 / Gini Coefficient

基尼系数根据洛伦兹曲线衡量分配不平等程度。它是摘要指标，无法完整描述分布结构。

The Gini coefficient summarizes distributional inequality using the Lorenz curve. As a summary measure, it does not fully describe the distribution.

### 23.5 绝对贫困与相对贫困 / Absolute and Relative Poverty

- 绝对贫困依据满足基本需求的固定或可比门槛。
- 相对贫困依据个人资源相对于社会中位水平的位置。

Absolute poverty uses a threshold tied to basic needs. Relative poverty measures resources compared with a society's median or typical level.

### 23.6 社会福利函数 / Social Welfare Function

社会福利函数用一种明确的价值判断，把不同个人的福利汇总为社会评价。

A social welfare function aggregates individual well-being into a social evaluation using explicit value judgments.

### 23.7 效率与公平 / Efficiency and Equity

效率关注资源是否产生最大可能总价值；公平关注收益、成本和机会如何分配。两者相关但不相同。

Efficiency asks whether resources generate the greatest feasible total value. Equity asks how benefits, costs, and opportunities are distributed. They are related but distinct.

### 23.8 代际公平 / Intergenerational Equity

代际公平关注当前决策对未来世代资源、债务、环境和机会的影响。

Intergenerational equity concerns how present decisions affect future generations' resources, debt, environment, and opportunities.

## 24. 经济思想主要流派 / Major Schools of Economic Thought

### 24.1 古典经济学 / Classical Economics

古典经济学强调分工、市场、生产、分配和长期增长，代表人物包括亚当·斯密和大卫·李嘉图。

Classical economics emphasizes specialization, markets, production, distribution, and long-run growth. Representative thinkers include Adam Smith and David Ricardo.

### 24.2 新古典经济学 / Neoclassical Economics

新古典经济学使用边际分析、优化和均衡研究消费者与企业选择。

Neoclassical economics uses marginal analysis, optimization, and equilibrium to study consumer and firm choices.

### 24.3 凯恩斯主义 / Keynesian Economics

凯恩斯主义强调短期价格或工资调整不完全、总需求波动和稳定政策的作用。

Keynesian economics emphasizes incomplete short-run price or wage adjustment, aggregate-demand fluctuations, and stabilization policy.

### 24.4 货币主义 / Monetarism

货币主义强调货币供给、通胀、稳定规则和政策时滞的重要性。

Monetarism emphasizes money, inflation, stable policy rules, and policy lags.

### 24.5 新古典宏观经济学 / New Classical Macroeconomics

新古典宏观经济学强调理性预期、市场出清、微观基础和政策规则。

New classical macroeconomics emphasizes rational expectations, market clearing, microfoundations, and policy rules.

### 24.6 新凯恩斯主义 / New Keynesian Economics

新凯恩斯主义使用微观基础解释价格和工资黏性、市场不完全与货币政策传导。

New Keynesian economics uses microfoundations to explain sticky prices and wages, market imperfections, and monetary-policy transmission.

### 24.7 奥地利学派 / Austrian School

奥地利学派强调主观价值、企业家发现、分散知识和市场过程。

The Austrian school emphasizes subjective value, entrepreneurial discovery, dispersed knowledge, and market processes.

### 24.8 马克思主义经济学 / Marxian Economics

马克思主义经济学关注生产关系、阶级、资本积累、利润和经济制度的历史变化。

Marxian economics examines relations of production, class, capital accumulation, profit, and historical change in economic systems.

### 24.9 制度经济学 / Institutional Economics

制度经济学强调法律、规范、组织、产权和历史路径对经济行为与绩效的影响。

Institutional economics emphasizes how laws, norms, organizations, property rights, and historical paths shape economic behavior and performance.

不同流派采用不同假设和研究重点。理解它们有助于识别模型的适用范围，而不是简单寻找唯一“正确流派”。

Schools differ in assumptions and emphasis. Understanding them helps identify model scope rather than reducing economics to a search for one universally correct school.

## 25. 贯穿经济学的核心原则 / Cross-Cutting Economic Principles

### 25.1 人们会对激励作出反应 / People Respond to Incentives

规则改变收益和成本，行为通常也会改变；政策评估应考虑直接反应和间接反应。

When rules change benefits and costs, behavior generally changes. Policy evaluation should consider direct and indirect responses.

### 25.2 没有免费的午餐 / There Is No Free Lunch

资源有机会成本。表面免费并不表示社会成本为零，只是成本可能由其他主体、其他时间或其他形式承担。

Resources have opportunity costs. A zero monetary price does not imply zero social cost; the cost may be borne by another party, at another time, or in another form.

### 25.3 价格传递信息 / Prices Convey Information

价格汇总稀缺程度、偏好、成本和预期等分散信息，并协调供需行为。

Prices aggregate dispersed information about scarcity, preferences, costs, and expectations and help coordinate supply and demand.

### 25.4 交易可以创造价值 / Trade Can Create Value

当主体的偏好、资源或机会成本不同时，自愿交易可以使双方受益。

When agents differ in preferences, resources, or opportunity costs, voluntary exchange can benefit both sides.

### 25.5 边际量决定选择 / Choices Depend on Margins

许多决策取决于额外一个单位的收益和成本，而不是历史平均值或沉没成本。

Many decisions depend on the benefit and cost of one additional unit, rather than historical averages or sunk costs.

### 25.6 沉没成本不应影响边际决策 / Sunk Costs Should Not Drive Marginal Decisions

已经发生且无法收回的成本不随当前选择变化，因此不应决定是否继续行动。

A cost already incurred and unrecoverable does not vary with the current choice and should not determine whether to continue.

### 25.7 短期与长期效果可能不同 / Short-Run and Long-Run Effects May Differ

行为、资本、技术和预期在长期有更多调整空间，因此同一政策的短期与长期结果可能不同。

Behavior, capital, technology, and expectations have more time to adjust in the long run, so a policy may have different short-run and long-run effects.

### 25.8 局部效果与一般均衡效果可能不同 / Partial and General-Equilibrium Effects May Differ

对单个市场看似合理的结论，在考虑其他市场价格、收入和反馈后可能改变。

A conclusion that appears valid in one market may change after accounting for prices, income, and feedback across other markets.

### 25.9 制度塑造激励 / Institutions Shape Incentives

产权、法律、执行、规范和组织形式会影响投资、交易、创新与合作。

Property rights, laws, enforcement, norms, and organizational forms shape investment, exchange, innovation, and cooperation.

### 25.10 效率不等于公平 / Efficiency Is Not the Same as Equity

有效率的配置可以高度不平等；公平判断也不能只由效率指标决定。

An efficient allocation may be highly unequal, and judgments about equity cannot be determined by efficiency measures alone.

### 25.11 模型结论依赖假设 / Model Conclusions Depend on Assumptions

经济学结论通常是“在这些条件下”的结论。应用模型前应检查机制、数据和制度背景。

Economic conclusions are usually conditional: they hold under specified assumptions. Application requires checking mechanisms, data, and institutional context.

## 26. 常见概念对照 / Common Concept Comparisons

### 26.1 存量与流量 / Stock and Flow

| 概念 | 中文说明 | English Description |
|---|---|---|
| 存量 / Stock | 某一时点测量，如财富、债务 | Measured at a point in time, such as wealth or debt |

### 26.2 名义量与实际量 / Nominal and Real Variables

| 概念 | 中文说明 | English Description |
|---|---|---|
| 名义量 / Nominal | 按当期货币价格衡量 | Measured in current monetary prices |
| 实际量 / Real | 经价格水平调整 | Adjusted for changes in the price level |

### 26.3 需求与需求量 / Demand and Quantity Demanded

| 概念 | 中文说明 | English Description |
|---|---|---|
| 需求 / Demand | 整条价格—数量关系 | The entire price-quantity relationship |
| 需求量 / Quantity Demanded | 某一价格下的具体数量 | A specific quantity at a given price |

### 26.4 会计利润与经济利润 / Accounting and Economic Profit

| 概念 | 成本范围 / Cost Coverage |
|---|---|
| 会计利润 / Accounting Profit | 收入减显性成本 / Revenue minus explicit costs |
| 经济利润 / Economic Profit | 收入减显性与隐性成本 / Revenue minus explicit and implicit costs |

### 26.5 经济增长与经济发展 / Growth and Development

| 概念 | 中文说明 | English Description |
|---|---|---|
| 增长 / Growth | 实际产出长期增加 | Sustained increase in real output |
| 发展 / Development | 收入、健康、教育、制度和能力综合改善 | Broader improvement in income, health, education, institutions, and capabilities |

### 26.6 风险与不确定性 / Risk and Uncertainty

| 概念 | 中文说明 | English Description |
|---|---|---|
| 风险 / Risk | 结果概率可估计 | Outcome probabilities can be estimated |
| 不确定性 / Uncertainty | 概率难以可靠确定 | Probabilities cannot be reliably specified |

### 26.7 平均量与边际量 / Average and Marginal Quantities

| 概念 | 中文说明 | English Description |
|---|---|---|
| 平均量 / Average | 总量除以单位数 | Total divided by number of units |
| 边际量 / Marginal | 增加一个单位带来的变化 | Change caused by one additional unit |

### 26.8 货币政策与财政政策 / Monetary and Fiscal Policy

| 概念 | 主要执行者 | 主要工具 |
|---|---|---|
| 货币政策 / Monetary Policy | 中央银行 / Central Bank | 利率、资产负债表、沟通 / Rates, balance sheet, communication |

## 28. 总结 / Summary

经济学并不只是研究金钱、股票或 GDP。它系统研究：

Economics is not merely the study of money, stocks, or GDP. It systematically examines:

- 人们如何在约束下选择 / How people choose under constraints;
- 价格如何协调分散决策 / How prices coordinate decentralized decisions;
- 企业如何组织生产和竞争 / How firms organize production and compete;
- 市场何时有效、何时失灵 / When markets work well and when they fail;
- 制度和信息如何塑造激励 / How institutions and information shape incentives;
- 总产出、就业和通胀如何变化 / How aggregate output, employment, and inflation change;
- 政策如何产生直接和间接效果 / How policy creates direct and indirect effects;
- 国家为什么贸易、增长或长期停滞 / Why economies trade, grow, or stagnate;
- 收入、财富、风险和机会如何分配 / How income, wealth, risk, and opportunity are distributed;
- 如何用数据区分相关、预测和因果 / How data distinguishes correlation, prediction, and causation.

贯穿经济学的核心思维是：

The core way of thinking across economics is:

> 在稀缺和约束下分析选择，识别机会成本和边际变化，理解激励与制度，追踪直接与间接反馈，并明确区分效率、分配与价值判断。
>
> Analyze choice under scarcity and constraints, identify opportunity costs and marginal changes, understand incentives and institutions, trace direct and indirect feedback, and clearly distinguish efficiency, distribution, and value judgments.
