# 化学核心原理 / Core Principles of Chemistry

## 学科定位 / Scope

化学研究物质的组成、结构、性质、变化以及变化过程中的能量关系。

Chemistry studies the composition, structure, properties, transformations of matter, and the energy changes accompanying those transformations.

## 知识地图 / Knowledge Map

```text
化学 Chemistry
├── 无机化学 Inorganic Chemistry
├── 有机化学 Organic Chemistry

├── 分析化学 Analytical Chemistry
├── 生物化学 Biochemistry
├── 材料化学 Materials Chemistry
└── 环境化学 Environmental Chemistry
```

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 原子 | Atom | 保留元素化学性质的基本单位；the basic unit retaining an element's chemical identity. |
| 元素 | Element | 由相同质子数原子组成的物质类别；a class of atoms with the same proton number. |
| 分子 | Molecule | 由化学键连接的原子集合；a group of atoms connected by chemical bonds. |
| 摩尔 | Mole | 表示固定数量微粒的物质的量单位；the amount-of-substance unit representing a fixed number of entities. |
| 化学键 | Chemical Bond | 使原子结合的相互作用；an interaction holding atoms together. |
| 化学反应 | Chemical Reaction | 原子重新组合形成新物质；rearrangement of atoms into new substances. |
| 化学计量 | Stoichiometry | 反应物与产物的定量关系；quantitative relationships among reactants and products. |
| 平衡 | Equilibrium | 正逆反应速率相等的动态状态；a dynamic state where forward and reverse rates are equal. |
| 反应速率 | Reaction Rate | 物质浓度随时间变化的速度；the rate at which concentration changes over time. |
| 活化能 | Activation Energy | 反应发生所需克服的能垒；the energy barrier that must be overcome for a reaction. |
| 酸碱 | Acid and Base | 质子或电子对转移相关物质；substances involved in proton or electron-pair transfer. |
| 氧化还原 | Redox | 涉及电子转移或氧化数变化的反应；reactions involving electron transfer or oxidation-state change. |
| 焓与自由能 | Enthalpy and Free Energy | 描述热效应与过程自发性的状态函数；state functions describing heat effects and spontaneity. |

## 基本原理 / Fundamental Principles

- 质量守恒 / Conservation of Mass
- 电荷守恒 / Conservation of Charge
- 元素周期律 / Periodic Law
- 结构决定性质 / Structure Determines Properties
- 勒夏特列原理 / Le Chatelier's Principle
- 热力学第一、第二定律 / First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics
- 反应速率受浓度、温度和催化剂影响 / Rates depend on concentration, temperature, and catalysts

## 研究方法 / Research Methods

- 合成 / Synthesis
- 分离与纯化 / Separation and Purification
- 滴定 / Titration
- 光谱 / Spectroscopy
- 色谱 / Chromatography
- 质谱 / Mass Spectrometry
- 结构表征 / Structural Characterization

## 关键对照 / Key Comparisons

- 物理变化与化学变化 / Physical vs. Chemical Change
- 离子键与共价键 / Ionic vs. Covalent Bond
- 动力学与热力学 / Kinetics vs. Thermodynamics
- 强度与浓度 / Strength vs. Concentration
- 精密度与准确度 / Precision vs. Accuracy

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

原子与周期表 → 化学键 → 化学计量 → 溶液与酸碱 → 热力学与动力学 → 有机、无机、分析和生物化学。

Atoms and periodic table → bonding → stoichiometry → solutions and acid-base chemistry → thermodynamics and kinetics → organic, inorganic, analytical, and biological chemistry.
