# 地球与空间科学核心原理 / Core Principles of Earth and Space Sciences

## 学科定位 / Scope

地球与空间科学研究地球系统、行星、恒星、星系和宇宙的形成、结构、过程与演化。

Earth and space sciences study the formation, structure, processes, and evolution of Earth systems, planets, stars, galaxies, and the universe.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 地球系统 | Earth System | 岩石圈、水圈、大气圈、生物圈相互作用；interaction of geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, and biosphere. |
| 深时 | Deep Time | 地质过程跨越极长时间尺度；geological processes unfold over immense timescales. |
| 板块构造 | Plate Tectonics | 岩石圈板块运动塑造地表；motion of lithospheric plates shapes Earth's surface. |
| 岩石循环 | Rock Cycle | 火成、沉积和变质岩相互转化；transformation among igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rocks. |
| 水循环 | Water Cycle | 水在大气、陆地、海洋和生物间循环；movement of water among atmosphere, land, oceans, and life. |
| 天气 | Weather | 短期大气状态；short-term atmospheric conditions. |
| 气候 | Climate | 较长时期天气的统计特征；long-term statistical characteristics of weather. |
| 辐射平衡 | Radiative Balance | 地球吸收与释放能量的平衡；balance between energy absorbed and emitted by Earth. |
| 轨道 | Orbit | 天体受引力约束的运动路径；a gravitationally bound path of a celestial body. |
| 恒星演化 | Stellar Evolution | 恒星随质量和核反应经历的生命周期；a star's lifecycle governed by mass and nuclear reactions. |
| 红移 | Redshift | 光谱向长波移动，可反映运动或宇宙膨胀；shift toward longer wavelengths indicating motion or cosmic expansion. |
| 宇宙膨胀 | Cosmic Expansion | 大尺度空间随时间扩展；large-scale expansion of space over time. |

## 基本原理 / Fundamental Principles

- 均变与突变共同塑造地球 / Gradual and catastrophic processes both shape Earth
- 现在是理解过去的重要钥匙 / Present processes help interpret the past
- 物质循环、能量流动 / Matter cycles and energy flows
- 系统之间存在反馈 / Earth systems contain feedback loops
- 引力支配大尺度天体运动 / Gravity governs large-scale celestial motion
- 光谱携带天体成分与运动信息 / Spectra reveal composition and motion

## 研究方法 / Research Methods

野外调查 / Fieldwork、遥感 / Remote Sensing、地球化学分析 / Geochemical Analysis、地震成像 / Seismic Imaging、数值模拟 / Numerical Modeling、望远镜观测 / Telescopic Observation、年代测定 / Geochronology.

## 关键对照 / Key Comparisons

- 天气与气候 / Weather vs. Climate
- 岩浆与熔岩 / Magma vs. Lava
- 恒星与行星 / Star vs. Planet
- 太阳系与星系 / Solar System vs. Galaxy
- 观测与模型 / Observation vs. Model

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

基础物理化学 → 地质与地球系统 → 大气海洋 → 行星与恒星 → 天体物理和宇宙学。

Basic physics and chemistry → geology and Earth systems → atmosphere and oceans → planets and stars → astrophysics and cosmology.
