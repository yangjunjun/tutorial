# 历史学与地理学核心原理 / Core Principles of History and Geography

## 学科定位 / Scope

历史学解释人类社会随时间的变化；地理学研究自然与人文现象在空间中的分布、关系和尺度。

History explains change in human societies over time. Geography studies the spatial distribution, relationships, and scales of physical and human phenomena.

## 知识地图 / Knowledge Map

政治史 / Political History、社会史 / Social History、经济史 / Economic History、思想文化史 / Intellectual and Cultural History、全球史 / Global History、自然地理 / Physical Geography、人文地理 / Human Geography、地图学与 GIS / Cartography and GIS.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 史料 | Historical Source | 用于认识过去的文字、物件、图像和口述证据；textual, material, visual, or oral evidence about the past. |
| 一手资料 | Primary Source | 由研究时期参与者或同时代产生的资料；evidence created by participants or contemporaries. |
| 二手资料 | Secondary Source | 后来的研究者对史料的分析；later scholarly analysis of sources. |
| 史境 | Historical Context | 事件所处的制度、观念和物质条件；institutional, intellectual, and material setting of an event. |
| 连续与变迁 | Continuity and Change | 历史中保持和转变的部分；what persists and what transforms over time. |
| 因果 | Causation | 多重条件如何共同产生历史结果；how multiple conditions contribute to outcomes. |
| 时段化 | Periodization | 为分析而划分历史阶段；division of history into analytical periods. |
| 空间 | Space | 位置、距离和分布的关系框架；a relational framework of location, distance, and distribution. |
| 地方 | Place | 被赋予意义的具体空间；a specific space endowed with meaning. |
| 尺度 | Scale | 从地方、区域到全球的分析层次；levels of analysis from local to global. |
| 区域 | Region | 按共同特征或功能划定的空间；an area defined by shared characteristics or functions. |
| 人地关系 | Human-Environment Interaction | 人类活动与自然环境的双向影响；reciprocal influence between human activity and nature. |

## 研究原则 / Research Principles

- 史料需要来源批判和交叉验证 / Sources require provenance criticism and corroboration.
- 不能用单一原因解释复杂历史 / Complex history rarely has a single cause.
- 避免以当代价值简单裁判过去 / Avoid simplistic presentism.
- 地图是经过选择的模型，不是中性现实复制 / Maps are selective models, not neutral copies of reality.
- 空间尺度变化可能改变结论 / Changing spatial scale may change conclusions.

## 研究方法 / Research Methods

档案研究 / Archival Research、口述史 / Oral History、比较史 / Comparative History、田野调查 / Fieldwork、遥感 / Remote Sensing、GIS 空间分析 / GIS Spatial Analysis、地图制图 / Cartography.

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

世界史与自然人文地理 → 史料和地图技能 → 区域专题 → 理论与方法 → 独立研究。

World history and physical/human geography → source and mapping skills → regional topics → theory and methods → independent research.
