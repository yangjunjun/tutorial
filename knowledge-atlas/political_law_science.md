# 政治学与法学核心原理 / Core Principles of Political Science and Law

## 学科定位 / Scope

政治学研究权力、国家、治理与集体决策；法学研究权利、义务、规则、解释和争议解决。

Political science studies power, states, governance, and collective decision-making. Law studies rights, duties, rules, interpretation, and dispute resolution.

## 知识地图 / Knowledge Map

政治理论 / Political Theory、比较政治 / Comparative Politics、国际关系 / International Relations、公共政策 / Public Policy、宪法 / Constitutional Law、民法 / Civil Law、刑法 / Criminal Law、行政法 / Administrative Law、国际法 / International Law.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 权力 | Power | 影响行为、议程或结果的能力；capacity to shape behavior, agendas, or outcomes. |
| 权威 | Authority | 被认为具有正当性的权力；power regarded as legitimate. |
| 国家 | State | 对领土和人口实施制度化统治的政治组织；a political organization exercising institutional rule over territory and population. |
| 主权 | Sovereignty | 最高统治权和政治独立性；supreme governing authority and political independence. |
| 合法性 | Legitimacy | 人们接受统治或规则正当性的程度；degree to which rule is accepted as rightful. |
| 民主 | Democracy | 公民参与选择和约束公共权力的制度；institutions allowing citizens to select and constrain public power. |
| 法治 | Rule of Law | 公权力与私人主体均受公开稳定规则约束；public and private actors are bound by public, stable rules. |
| 权利 | Right | 法律或道德认可的主张、自由或保障；a legally or morally recognized claim, liberty, or protection. |
| 义务 | Duty | 法律或规范要求履行或避免的行为；an action required or prohibited by law or norm. |
| 管辖权 | Jurisdiction | 机构审理、立法或执法的权限范围；scope of authority to adjudicate, legislate, or enforce. |
| 正当程序 | Due Process | 公权力作出不利决定时应遵循公平程序；fair procedures required for adverse public decisions. |
| 先例 | Precedent | 先前裁判对后续案件的指导或约束；guidance or constraint from prior decisions. |

## 核心原则 / Core Principles

- 权力需要来源、边界和问责 / Power requires sources, limits, and accountability.
- 分权与制衡降低权力集中风险 / Separation of powers and checks and balances limit concentration.
- 法律解释涉及文本、目的、体系与先例 / Legal interpretation uses text, purpose, structure, and precedent.
- 程序正义与实体正义相互关联 / Procedural and substantive justice are related.
- 政治制度塑造参与者激励 / Political institutions shape incentives.
