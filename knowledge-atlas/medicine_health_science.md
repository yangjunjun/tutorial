# 医学与健康科学核心原理 / Core Principles of Medicine and Health Sciences

## 学科定位 / Scope

医学与健康科学研究人体结构与功能、疾病机制、预防诊断治疗，以及个人和人群健康。

Medicine and health sciences study human structure and function, disease mechanisms, prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and the health of individuals and populations.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 解剖 | Anatomy | 人体结构及其空间关系；body structures and their spatial relationships. |
| 生理 | Physiology | 正常生命功能及其调节；normal body functions and their regulation. |
| 病理 | Pathology | 疾病导致的结构和功能变化；structural and functional changes caused by disease. |
| 病因 | Etiology | 疾病发生的原因和条件；causes and conditions of disease. |
| 稳态 | Homeostasis | 内部环境的动态稳定；dynamic stability of the internal environment. |
| 诊断 | Diagnosis | 根据证据识别疾病或状态；identification of a disease or condition from evidence. |
| 鉴别诊断 | Differential Diagnosis | 系统比较可能解释症状的疾病；systematic comparison of conditions that could explain symptoms. |
| 治疗 | Treatment | 改善病因、病程、症状或功能的干预；an intervention addressing cause, course, symptoms, or function. |
| 预后 | Prognosis | 对疾病未来过程和结局的估计；an estimate of future course and outcome. |
| 风险因素 | Risk Factor | 与疾病发生概率增加相关的特征；a characteristic associated with increased disease probability. |
| 发病率 | Incidence | 一定时期内新发病例频率；frequency of new cases over a period. |
| 患病率 | Prevalence | 某时点或时期现有病例比例；proportion with a condition at a time or over a period. |
| 敏感度 | Sensitivity | 检出真正患病者的能力；ability to identify true cases. |
| 特异度 | Specificity | 正确识别未患病者的能力；ability to identify true non-cases. |

## 核心原则 / Core Principles

- 循证医学 / Evidence-Based Medicine：结合最佳证据、临床经验和患者价值。
- 预防优先 / Prevention：一级、二级和三级预防。
- 风险收益权衡 / Risk-Benefit Balance。
- 知情同意 / Informed Consent。
- 不伤害与行善 / Nonmaleficence and Beneficence。
- 公平 / Justice：合理分配健康资源。
- 个体差异 / Individual Variation：治疗应考虑患者背景。

## 研究方法 / Research Methods

- 病例报告 / Case Report
- 横断面研究 / Cross-Sectional Study
- 病例对照研究 / Case-Control Study
- 队列研究 / Cohort Study
- 随机对照试验 / Randomized Controlled Trial
- 系统综述与荟萃分析 / Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

## 关键对照 / Key Comparisons

- 症状与体征 / Symptom vs. Sign
- 筛查与诊断 / Screening vs. Diagnosis
- 发病率与患病率 / Incidence vs. Prevalence
- 相对风险与绝对风险 / Relative vs. Absolute Risk
- 统计显著与临床重要 / Statistical Significance vs. Clinical Importance

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

生物与化学 → 解剖、生理、生化 → 病理、微生物、药理 → 临床诊断治疗 → 公共卫生与循证实践。

Biology and chemistry → anatomy, physiology, and biochemistry → pathology, microbiology, and pharmacology → clinical diagnosis and treatment → public health and evidence-based practice.
