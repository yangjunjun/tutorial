# 管理学与金融核心原理 / Core Principles of Management and Finance

## 学科定位 / Scope

管理学研究组织如何制定目标、配置资源和协调行动；金融研究资金跨时间和风险状态的配置。

Management studies how organizations set goals, allocate resources, and coordinate action. Finance studies the allocation of funds across time and states of risk.

## 知识地图 / Knowledge Map

战略 / Strategy、组织行为 / Organizational Behavior、运营 / Operations、市场营销 / Marketing、人力资源 / Human Resources、会计 / Accounting、公司金融 / Corporate Finance、投资 / Investments、风险管理 / Risk Management、创业 / Entrepreneurship.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

| 中文概念 | English Term | 双语说明 / Bilingual Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 组织 | Organization | 为共同目标协调人员和资源的系统；a system coordinating people and resources toward shared goals. |
| 战略 | Strategy | 面对竞争和不确定性的长期选择体系；a coherent set of long-term choices under competition and uncertainty. |
| 商业模式 | Business Model | 组织如何创造、交付和获取价值；how an organization creates, delivers, and captures value. |
| 利益相关者 | Stakeholder | 影响组织或受组织影响的主体；a party affecting or affected by an organization. |
| 竞争优势 | Competitive Advantage | 使组织持续创造相对更高价值的能力；capacity to create superior value over time. |

## 核心原则 / Core Principles

- 战略意味着选择做什么和不做什么 / Strategy requires choices about what to do and not do.
- 指标会影响行为，需防止目标替代 / Metrics influence behavior and may become poor substitutes for goals.
- 利润不等于现金流 / Profit is not the same as cash flow.
- 风险与预期收益需要共同评估 / Risk and expected return must be assessed together.
- 沉没成本不应决定未来投资 / Sunk costs should not determine future investment.
- 治理需要平衡授权、监督和责任 / Governance balances delegation, oversight, and accountability.

## 研究与实践方法 / Methods

案例分析 / Case Analysis、市场研究 / Market Research、财务报表分析 / Financial Statement Analysis、估值 / Valuation、运营建模 / Operations Modeling、实验与 A/B 测试 / Experiments and A/B Tests、情景规划 / Scenario Planning.

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

经济与会计基础 → 组织、营销和运营 → 公司金融与投资 → 战略和治理 → 行业项目。

Economics and accounting → organization, marketing, and operations → corporate finance and investment → strategy and governance → industry projects.
