# 环境与农业科学核心原理 / Core Principles of Environmental and Agricultural Sciences

## 学科定位 / Scope

环境科学研究自然系统与人类活动的相互影响；农业科学研究食物、纤维和其他生物资源的可持续生产。

Environmental science studies interactions between natural systems and human activity. Agricultural science studies sustainable production of food, fiber, and other biological resources.

## 知识地图 / Knowledge Map

生态 / Ecology、气候 / Climate、污染控制 / Pollution Control、保护生物学 / Conservation Biology、农学 / Agronomy、土壤学 / Soil Science、作物学 / Crop Science、动物科学 / Animal Science、林业 / Forestry、食品科学 / Food Science.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

## 核心原则 / Core Principles

- 物质循环而能量单向流动 / Matter cycles while energy flows.
- 环境问题具有系统反馈和时间滞后 / Environmental problems involve feedback and time lags.
- 预防污染通常优于末端治理 / Pollution prevention is often preferable to end-of-pipe treatment.
- 农业需权衡产量、资源、韧性和生态影响 / Agriculture balances yield, resources, resilience, and ecological impact.
- 可持续性包含环境、经济和社会维度 / Sustainability has environmental, economic, and social dimensions.

## 学习路径 / Learning Path

生物化学与地球科学 → 生态和土壤 → 环境监测与农业系统 → 政策经济和管理 → 专业方向。

Biology, chemistry, and Earth science → ecology and soil → environmental monitoring and agricultural systems → policy, economics, and management → specialization.
